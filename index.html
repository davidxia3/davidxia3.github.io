Reinforcement learning, agents & AI for science

I'm a senior at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign studying computer science, mathematics, and statistics. My current interests lie in AI for science, especially agents that learn to search and design using costly, imperfect evaluations. I'm applying to PhD programs for fall 2027, with broader interests in empirical reinforcement learning and language model agents.

About

I'm interested in how an agent chooses what to test and which evaluations to trust. My current focus is on multifidelity optimization and reinforcement learning for allocating a limited budget between experiments, model improvement, and planning. More broadly, I'm interested in empirical reinforcement learning and language model agents, including exploration, tool use, and learning strategies that transfer across tasks.
