iDEA-iSAIL Lab Feb 2026 – present
Advised by Prof. Hanghang Tong
  • Studied sample-efficient exploration and function approximation in reinforcement learning, focusing on Markov decision processes with linearly representable policy values.
  • Developed a manuscript extending Frozen Policy Iteration to stochastic transitions under linear Qπ realizability, with a sample-complexity guarantee under an additional coverage assumption.
  • Currently investigating an algorithm for deterministic MDPs with improved sample-complexity guarantees, while extending the analysis to broader settings.
